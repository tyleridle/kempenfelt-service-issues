Service / Install Issue Report

For Kempenfelt sales reps: report a service or install issue for a customer who has had windows or doors installed.

How this works

  1. Fill in and submit — your report saves straight to the managers’ tracker sheet. Submit one form per customer issue.
  2. Then tap “Send summary to WhatsApp” — WhatsApp opens with the summary already written. Pick the Kempenfelt Service Issues group and tap Send, so it posts from you.
  3. Photos? Email them to the service team and tick “Photos Submitted?” below, so they know to check their email. No photos are uploaded on this form.

Please don’t report the same issue by phone, text, or email as well — one list keeps everyone organized.
